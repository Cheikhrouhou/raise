RAISE Competition

The RAISE 2025 competition aims to foster innovation and practical skills in robotics and AI.

Tracks:

  • Track 1 (Autonomous Robot): Follow a 10-meter black path (5 cm wide) autonomously.
  • Track 2 (Industrial Robot): Carry a 5cm³ box and follow a black path for 10 meters.
  • Track 3 (Agriculture Robot): Read QR codes with a camera and act (FORWARD, BACKWARD, etc.) along a 10-meter path.

Rewards:

  • Monetary prizes for each track winner
  • Potential industrial partnerships for real-world implementation
